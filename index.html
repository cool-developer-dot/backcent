What we find

What we find.

The small errors that add up.

Most finance teams believe their three-way match catches everything. It catches most things. The rest is small, scattered and invisible until someone looks at three years of data at once.

Duplicate payments

Same invoice paid twice under two numbers, two vendors, or two systems.

Unused vendor credits

Credit notes your suppliers issued that you never took.

Statement gaps

Your ledger vs. the supplier’s statement. The difference is usually yours.

Pricing and contract errors

Invoiced above the agreed price, rebate, or volume tier.

Missed early-pay discounts

Terms you earned and didn’t take.

Tax, freight and surcharges

Tax on exempt items; fuel, accessorial and rate-card overbilling.

Typical claim mix in a first audit

Industry pattern, not a promise. This is why we start with supplier statements, not duplicates.

  • ~40% Returns and credits
  • ~20% Pricing
  • <20% Duplicates
  • Rest Discounts, tax and freight
